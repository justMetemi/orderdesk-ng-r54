ORDERDESK NG R4.9 — FINAL STORE VISIBILITY, PRODUCT REVIEWS & AI ASSISTANTS
==========================================================================

This review package consolidates R4.7 store discovery/sharing, R4.8 AI assistants,
and the additional product ratings/customer feedback requested for R4.9.
No live Netlify deployment or Supabase database changes were performed.

STORE VISIBILITY FEATURES RETAINED FROM R4.7
1. Public store page has a visible “Share store” button in the storefront hero area.
2. Merchant dashboard > Online Store has “Copy link” beside the public shop link.
3. Each product detail modal has “Share product” and “Copy product link” buttons.
4. Product deep links use the shop URL plus ?product=<product-id> and open that exact product.
5. Store/product page title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter metadata and JSON-LD are updated dynamically.
6. Store setup includes guidance explaining how the shop name, tagline and description contribute to search/share metadata.

IMPORTANT: The share controls are in the locations above; they are not a global setting called “Enable Share”.
Metadata is client-rendered after the page loads. Some social crawlers may not execute JavaScript, so
link preview/indexing behavior is not guaranteed. Server-side/prerendered metadata would be a separate upgrade.

NEW PRODUCT RATINGS & CUSTOMER FEEDBACK
- Product cards display an average star rating and review count (or “No reviews yet”).
- Product detail modal includes a customer rating and feedback section.
- Customers submit name, email, a 1–5 star rating and written feedback.
- One review per email address per product is enforced by a database unique constraint.
- Reviews are published immediately and are publicly visible; do not describe them as verified purchases.
- Customer email is stored for duplicate prevention but is not returned in public review results.
- Public review reads and submissions use narrowly scoped SECURITY DEFINER RPCs; direct anon/authenticated table access is revoked and RLS is enabled.

REQUIRED DATABASE STEP FOR R4.9 REVIEWS
1. Open the OrderDesk NG Supabase project (not TEMI's separate project).
2. Open SQL Editor.
3. Run orderdesk_product_reviews_migration.sql.
4. Confirm it completes successfully before testing reviews.
No existing tables are dropped or altered by this migration; it adds a separate review table and two RPCs.
Do not run it in the TEMI project. Back up the database first as a general precaution.

AI ASSISTANTS RETAINED FROM R4.8
- Merchant AI is available in the merchant dashboard and merchant Online Store area.
- It uses authenticated merchant access and existing RLS for merchant data.
- It can read products, orders, stock and sales summaries and propose approved actions.
- Public shopper assistant only receives public shop/product information and cannot access merchant records or mutate data.
- Existing GROQ_CHATBOT_API_KEY and GROQ_HELP_MODEL settings are reused.
- Sensitive payment actions, refunds, deleting records and subscription changes remain restricted.
