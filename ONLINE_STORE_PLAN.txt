ONLINE STORE — IMPLEMENTED SCOPE

Owner:
- Configure store name/description/slug/status/delivery fee/bank details.
- Publish/hide existing products.
- Review online orders and line items.
- Confirm/reject transfer receipts.
- Update online order status.
- Open private receipts through signed URLs.

Customer:
- Browse/search/filter published products.
- Add multiple products to cart and change quantities.
- Checkout with name, phone, optional email/note and delivery address.
- Pay by bank transfer with receipt upload or pay on delivery.
- Receive unique order reference.
- Track using order reference + phone.

Security:
- Public storefront uses narrow RPCs; no anonymous table SELECT.
- Database recalculates prices/totals from Products.
- Product rows are locked and stock is reserved atomically at checkout.
- Duplicate submissions use an idempotency key.
- Online order stock is restored on cancellation and re-reserved if reopened.
- Public access is blocked after trial/subscription expiry; existing owner records remain accessible.
